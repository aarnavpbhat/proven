Cedar Valley Family Medicine - October 2026 expense variance memo

Total operating expenses were $401.6K, up 9.7% from September. Visits were 4,180 for the month, up 6%, and September collections of $312.6K were our best this year.

Staffing. Locum costs of $28.8K are new this month, covering Dr. Alvarez's leave. Provider compensation was down about $18K with Dr. Alvarez on leave. Clinical wages were up 5% on overtime for the Saturday flu clinics. Payroll taxes and benefits eased about $1.2K with Dr. Alvarez off payroll.

Clinical. Vaccines were up $13K as the second, larger flu pre-book shipment landed in October. Lab fees fell $12.5K, entirely the clean-up of September's duplicate Valley Reference invoice. Medical supplies were up $3.1K on the new sharps disposal program.

Admin and occupancy. Billing service fees rose $2.3K due to the clearinghouse price increase. EHR and IT were up roughly $3.6K: the one-time patient portal implementation, net of a service credit for September's network outage. Med mal rose $770 on the October 1 renewal. CME rose $3.2K for Dr. Kim's trip to the FMX conference. We will budget locum coverage through mid-January.
