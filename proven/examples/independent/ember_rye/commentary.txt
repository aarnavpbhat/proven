Ember & Rye Hospitality Group - July 2026 P&L flash, cost lines vs June

Total costs for the group came in at $468.6K, up 27% on June. Sales were strong across all three rooms with patio season in full swing, and covers were up 11%.

Food cost rose about $3K, or 4%, on higher patio covers, net of a $1.8K Evergreen credit for product lost when the Fremont walk-in failed. Beverage cost was up $3.1K, driven by the rosé promotion. Hourly labor was up $65K, driven by patio season staffing and July 4th overtime. Payroll taxes & benefits rose $6.2K (30%) because July had three pay dates against two in June.

R&M rose $8.1K because of the Fremont walk-in compressor replacement. Utilities came down $1.1K as gas usage eased for the summer. Occupancy was flat at $41.3K. Linen was up $4.1K, but that's timing: Unitex's June invoice for Ballard posted in July instead of June.

3PD commissions climbed ~$2.2K after we moved Capitol Hill to DashCart's Premier tier. Marketing rose $2.4K on our Restaurant Week listing. Entertainment & events up about $6K for the July 4th block party in Ballard (band, stage and street permit). We'll revisit the Premier tier in September once we have two months of order data.
