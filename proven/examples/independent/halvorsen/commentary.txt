Halvorsen Precision - Sept '26 operating cost variances vs Aug

Total spend was up $108.9K (30%) to $473.1K. Shipments to Aerovane started on 9/22 and first article inspection passed; on-time delivery was 91% and scrap fell to 2.1%.

Materials & labor: Raw materials were up $42.8K (44%), mainly the Aerovane bracket program ramp, net of a $7.2K credit for out-of-tolerance bar. Direct labor rose about 10% as we built out the second shift for Aerovane. Outside processing roughly doubled, mostly anodizing on the Aerovane brackets.

Plant: Machine maintenance jumped $18.3K: the Mill 3 spindle rebuild plus a full coolant system flush. Shop supplies were up $3.9K for the coolant flush. Plant utilities rose $3.4K on summer peak-demand charges. Quality costs rose $11.6K for the AS9100 surveillance audit.

Freight: Inbound freight rose $6.5K because we had to air-freight stainless bar after the mill shipped late. Outbound freight was down about $1.5K on fewer customer shipments.
