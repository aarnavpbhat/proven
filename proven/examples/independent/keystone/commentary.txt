KEYSTONE MECHANICAL - SEPTEMBER 2026 COST REVIEW (vs AUG)

Total costs were $490K for the month, up 16%. Mercy Ridge is now 38% complete and backlog stands at $6.2M.

Job costs. Materials were up $54K, primarily the three rooftop units for Mercy Ridge, partly offset by the copper return credit. Subcontractor costs fell ~$30K as the August insulation accrual reversed. Field labor rose about 5% on overtime to hit the Mercy Ridge set date. Equipment rental rose ~$10K on the crane pick at Mercy Ridge; a second pick is scheduled for October.

Fleet. Fuel rose $3.5K on higher diesel prices. Vehicle repairs rose $6.9K, mostly the transmission rebuild on Truck 14 (reclassed out of small tools), and small tools were down about $1.1K.

Overhead. Permits and inspections were up $2.2K, the Mercy Ridge mechanical permit. Insurance jumped $11.3K on the workers' comp audit true-up for the 2025-26 policy year. Safety & training up $5.3K for the foremen's OSHA 30 class.
