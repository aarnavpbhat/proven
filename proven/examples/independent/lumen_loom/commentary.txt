Lumen & Loom - October 2026 month-end commentary

October revenue was $1.48M, up 24%, with Hearth at 31% of sales; gross margin held at 61%. Opex came in at $299.2K, up 15%.

Marketing. Paid social was up ~72% as we put spend behind the Hearth fall launch on SocialReach and Clipstream, funded in part by pulling about $4K out of search. Affiliate & influencer spend rose $5.2K, mostly FX on the euro-denominated creator retainer. Creative fell $12.7K as the Hearth shoot was a September cost. We expect social to stay elevated through Black Friday.

Operations. 3PL costs rose $7.5K, driven by storage as we built inventory ahead of Q4 (412 pallets vs. 286 in September). Outbound shipping rose $6.2K, largely the carrier demand surcharges that kicked in this month. Returns costs fell $2.3K thanks to the self-service returns portal we launched. Processing fees were up ~$1K with the higher sales.

Tech. Platform and apps rose $6K on the annual renewal of our on-site search app.
