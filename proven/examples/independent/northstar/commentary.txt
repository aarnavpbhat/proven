Northstar Freightways - October 2026 operating expense commentary

Total operating expenses rose 18% to $754K. Loaded miles were up 6.4% to 1.21M, revenue per loaded mile held at $2.71, and the operating ratio improved to 93.8%.

Fuel rose ~$19.5K (about 12%) on higher diesel prices and the extra Winnipeg miles. Driver wages were up 6% on the higher mileage. Purchased transportation was up 25%, or about $40K, as we brokered out the peak overflow on the Harlow Foods account.

Equipment: Maintenance came in about $11K lower: September carried the in-frame overhaul on unit 2207, partly offset by this month's DPF cleaning program. Tires were up about $10K on the winter steer-tire program (14 tractors). Dispatch software was up $3.3K with the trailer trackers going live.

Risk and people: Insurance increased $5.6K, all of it the October 1 renewal of the auto liability and cargo program. Cargo claims rose $12.4K on the Harlow Foods water damage claim. Driver recruiting rose $7.6K on the expanded job-board campaign. Tolls and permits were flat at about $9K.
