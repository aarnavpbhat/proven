Riverbend Community Food Network - June 2026 (FY26 year-end) expense variance report to the Finance Committee

Total expenses were $300.7K for June, up 35% over May. We distributed 1.9 million pounds of food in FY26, a record, and the board approved the FY27 budget of $4.1M on June 24.

Programs. Food purchases rose $18K, reflecting the produce price increases we flagged last quarter. Grants to partner agencies rose $48K with the Q4 disbursement to our six partner pantries. Program salaries were up about 13% on seasonal summer meals staff. Fleet costs rose about $5.5K, nearly all of it the reefer repair on Truck 3. Volunteer hours were 3,420 in June.

Fundraising. Special events were down $26.6K with the gala behind us in May; the $4.2K Fireside balance that came in this month is the gala's final bill. Printing and postage rose $3.2K on the gala invitations.

Administration. Professional fees were up $18K, the year-end accrual for the FY26 audit. Warehouse occupancy fell $2.2K on the landlord's CAM reconciliation credit. Technology rose $4.4K for the new donor CRM.
