Tidewater Coffee Roasters: October 2026 close, operating expense commentary.
Total operating expenses rose $43.1K (57%) in October to $119.1K.
Freight and delivery rose $10.6K, mainly due to the holiday gift box shipments.
Roaster maintenance increased $11.9K because of the drum bearing replacement on roaster #2.
Utilities rose $3.4K on higher gas usage as the weather turned cold.
Professional services fell $6.4K, mainly because the September trademark office action response did not recur.
Packaging rose $10.3K due to the switch to compostable bags.
Marketing rose $8.8K, driven by the Cascadia cyclists sponsorship.
Software and IT spend rose $2.1K because of the new Cin7 inventory system.
Rent and occupancy was flat at $10.4K.
Insurance rose $1.2K due to the annual premium audit adjustment.
We expect freight to stay elevated through the holiday season.
